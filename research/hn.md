## Cross-thread recurrence (what to build against)

- **Save/track-anything family (#1, #2, #3, #17, #23)** — appears in ≥6 distinct high-point Ask HN threads across 2018→2026. The complaint is never "I can't save", it is "I can't find it again / it makes me anxious".
- **Password form hostility (#4, #5)** — 2 sub-complaints in one 564-pt thread + a separate 2023 autofill thread. Very static-tool-friendly (no backend needed).
- **Billing/subscription dark patterns (#6, #7, #8, #9)** — strongest *consumer* recurrence: 6+ stories over 100 pts, spanning FTC (2023/2024/2025), California law (2018), enforcement (2026).
- **Date/time & timestamp conversion (#12, #13)** — 3+ asks inside a single thread; zero infrastructure; classic "small tool" winner.
- **Noise from automation (#10 dependency bots, #14 compose YAML, #22 Jira)** — recurring theme: tools that generate more work than they save.
