**Bottom line:** The single most-repeated *feature* ask is **automatic resurfacing of forgotten saves (random/digest/"remind me") to defeat the unread pile** — 10 distinct issues across every major reader, still open after a decade (wallabag #2285 from 2016, karakeep #435/#705 from 2024, miniflux #1493). The single most *intense* driver is **fear of the tool dying** — corroborated live by Pocket's own site ("phase out Pocket") and by the Omnivore collapse (141-reaction GitHub backlash + a 1222-point HN thread), which converts the "graveyard" from a feeling into a proven risk. Users want BOTH halves of the product pitch: resurfacing (finish the pile) and durability/portability (so it doesn't vanish) — the two are inseparable in the evidence: people hoard links, never read them, then the tool dies and the pile is lost anyway (dylan604, a20eac1d, _def).

Caveats: readeck contributed no GitHub data (issues disabled); miniflux/wallabag are RSS/aggregator-adjacent, not pure bookmark managers; Omnivore's own blog and Mozilla's KB page were **not** text-retrievable this session, so any precise shutdown date remains UNVERIFIED.
